# 🏥 MediShield AI

<div align="center">

### Explainable Preventive Healthcare Intelligence Platform

Predict • Explain • Prevent

An AI-powered healthcare platform that combines Disease Risk Prediction, Explainable AI (XAI), Medical Report Analysis, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Intelligent Healthcare Agents to deliver transparent and personalized preventive healthcare insights.

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Healthcare systems across the world are primarily reactive.

Most patients seek medical attention only after symptoms become severe, increasing treatment complexity, healthcare costs, and long-term health risks.

Additionally, modern AI-powered healthcare tools often suffer from:

* Lack of explainability
* Black-box predictions
* Poor patient trust
* Fragmented medical information
* Limited preventive guidance

As a result, users receive predictions but rarely understand:

* Why the prediction was made
* Which health factors influenced the outcome
* What actions can reduce future risk

---

## 💡 Our Solution

MediShield AI transforms healthcare from **reactive treatment** to **preventive intelligence**.

The platform combines advanced machine learning, explainable AI, medical knowledge retrieval, and conversational healthcare agents into a unified ecosystem that helps users:

* Understand their health risks
* Analyze symptoms
* Interpret medical reports
* Receive evidence-based recommendations
* Learn preventive healthcare practices

# 🚀 Core Features

## 🩺 Disease Risk Prediction

Advanced machine learning models predict the likelihood of:

### Diabetes

Analyze clinical indicators and lifestyle factors to estimate diabetes risk.

### Heart Disease

Identify cardiovascular risk factors using healthcare datasets and predictive models.

### Stroke Risk

Estimate stroke susceptibility based on demographic and medical parameters.

---

## 📊 Explainable AI (XAI)

Unlike traditional black-box systems, MediShield AI explains every prediction.

### Explainability Features

* SHAP Explanations
* LIME Explanations
* Feature Contribution Analysis
* Global Feature Importance
* Personalized Risk Interpretation

Users can understand:

* Why a prediction was generated
* Which health factors contributed most
* How lifestyle improvements may affect future outcomes

---

## 🤖 AI Symptom Checker

Users can describe symptoms using natural language.

The system provides:

* Potential conditions
* Risk assessments
* Confidence scores
* Preventive guidance
* Suggested next actions

---

## 📄 Medical Report Intelligence

Upload laboratory reports and medical documents for automated analysis.

### Capabilities

* OCR Extraction
* Medical Text Processing
* Report Summarization
* Abnormality Detection
* Health Risk Identification

Supported formats:

* PDF Reports
* Lab Reports
* Scanned Medical Documents

An intelligent healthcare assistant capable of:

* Explaining predictions

---

## 📚 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

To reduce hallucinations and improve trustworthiness, MediShield AI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Before generating responses, the system retrieves relevant information from trusted medical sources.

### Knowledge Sources

* WHO Guidelines
* CDC Publications
* NIH Research Resources
* Verified Healthcare Literature

This enables evidence-backed healthcare responses rather than generic AI-generated answers.
